# 30-second promo

Show the app on a phone at a real counter. Match the spoken language and captions. Do not show a plan price or checkout; plans are managed on the website.

| Time | Picture | English | Hindi |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4s | QR stand and NFC card on a counter; a customer scans, then taps | One page. Scan or tap. | एक पेज। स्कैन करें या टैप। |
| 4–9s | Home dashboard with today's activity and the setup guide | See what happened today, and what to do next. | आज की गतिविधि और अगला कदम देखें। |
| 9–15s | Customer page opens: menu and services, followed by a WhatsApp order and booking | Show your menu and services. Take orders and booking requests on WhatsApp. | मेनू और सेवाएँ दिखाएँ। WhatsApp पर ऑर्डर और बुकिंग लें। |
| 15–20s | Owner moves an order forward, then reads feedback and a review | Keep orders moving. Read feedback and reviews in one place. | ऑर्डर आगे बढ़ाएँ। फ़ीडबैक और समीक्षा एक जगह पढ़ें। |
| 20–25s | Agent home and team summary; agent shares an invite | Onboard businesses and follow your team's sales. | व्यवसाय जोड़ें और अपनी टीम की बिक्री देखें। |
| 25–30s | Swipe through theme picker, QR and NFC screen, then finish on green logo | Eight themes. QR and NFC. QR24x7. Scan. Tap. Grow. | आठ थीम। QR और NFC। QR24x7. Scan. Tap. Grow. |

End card: QR24x7 logo on brand green, with “Scan. Tap. Grow.”
